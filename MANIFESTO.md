# Why sign — and what signing does *not* mean

*Companion note to the signatory campaign for the Proclamation of Individual
Equity (DOI [10.5281/zenodo.21397274](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21397274)).*

## What this is

This is a campaign to let individuals — and institutions — place a
**cryptographically verifiable, decentralised signature** beside the canonical
Proclamation of Individual Equity. A signature here is an OpenTimestamps proof:
a timestamp anchored to a public blockchain that shows a named or pseudonymous
party endorsed the exact, immutable text at a specific moment. No central
authority issues it; no central authority can revoke it.

The purpose is **preservation** — of identity and individuality in the age of
advanced AI. As increasingly capable systems mediate language, culture, and
thought, the argument of the Proclamation is that the irreducible fingerprint of
each mind — its idiolect, its internal architecture, its intellectual footprint —
deserves recognition rather than flattening into a statistical norm. Signing is a
way to record, verifiably and permanently, that one stands for that recognition.

## What this is **not**

**This is not an anti-institution movement.** It opposes no university, no
company, no government, no journal, no standards body. It does not ask signatories
to withdraw from, boycott, or distrust any institution. The Proclamation's target
is *cognitive domination* and *flattening metrics* — the reduction of distinct
minds to a defined "normality" — not institutions as such. Institutions are
explicitly invited to sign **as institutions**; the registry has a first-class
place for them (`capacity: institution`).

- It is **not** a petition, a legal instrument, or a demand on any body.
- It is **not** a claim of authority over anyone, nor a membership, party, or creed.
- It does **not** harvest data. A signatory submits only what they choose — a name
  or pseudonym, an optional affiliation, an optional link. Anonymity is permitted.
- It does **not** replace or compete with formal scholarly or legal recognition;
  it **complements** them by anchoring individual provenance in a way no single
  party controls. Curated collections of these atomic signatures can later be
  published through formal channels (a Zenodo DOI via Misty DOI) — the institutional
  and the individual layers reinforce each other rather than oppose.

## The balance

The Proclamation itself asks that equity be pursued "while maintaining Balance of
Interpretation." That balance applies here too: this campaign affirms the individual
without denying the collective, and records dissent-proof provenance without
asserting power over anyone. It is an act of preservation, offered in service of the
Human Collective — not against any part of it.
